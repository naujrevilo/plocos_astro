Do not fear your own nature, everything happens for a reason. Show your nature for everyone to see, but be careful not to scare the viewer. You will be surprise how many learn, grow and accept. Always be nice, good nature and decent about it. Do not let despair take control of your actions. Project your nature toward happiness, love, sharing and caring. Give it all in life, do not let it go to waste.
