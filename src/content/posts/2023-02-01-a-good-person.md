Your decency not your status,
Your kindness not your handouts,
Your character not your looks,
Your sincere greeting not your arrogance,
Your patience not your intransigence,
Your fortitude, not your strength,
Your faith and love not your religion,
Your firmness not your stubbornness,
Your serenity not your anxiety,
Your ambition not your greed,
Your wisdom not what you know,
Your courage not your fear,

Your elegance not your tawdrines,
Will make you a good leader, or,
a good follower, or,
simply a good person.
